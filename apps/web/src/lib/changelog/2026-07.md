#### Smoother performance on older devices

Table Slayer now adapts its rendering quality to your hardware. A new performance setting in the play controls defaults to Auto, which watches your frame rate and automatically lowers quality — resolution, fog detail, weather density, and drawing effects — when a device can't keep up. You can also pin the quality to High, Medium, or Low manually. [#463](https://github.com/Siege-Perilous/tableslayer/pull/463)

"Map defined" now supports maps that are larger (in grid units) than your available TV space. The grid, tokens, lights, fog, and drawings are now anchored directly to the map, so panning the map moves everything together. Instead of being limited to just the TV viewport, the editor now shows your entire map with a white outline marking exactly what's visible on the playfield. Hold SHIFT + mouse drag to move the view, or use a four-finger drag on touch devices. [#468](https://github.com/Siege-Perilous/tableslayer/pull/468)

#### Polygon fog rooms

<video controls width="100%">

  Your browser does not support the video tag.

A new fog room tool (P) lets you outline rooms of fog point by point, perfect for prepping a dungeon ahead of a session. Click to place each corner, then press Enter, double-click, or click the first point to complete the room. Committed rooms are immune to the eraser, so revealing a hallway can't accidentally bleed into the next chamber. Check the shortcuts' helper in the editor for usage. [#471](https://github.com/Siege-Perilous/tableslayer/pull/471)
