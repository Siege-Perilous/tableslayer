#### Small features

- Real-time sync moved from PartyKit to Cloudflare's PartyServer, the same platform under a new runtime. Rooms now hibernate while nobody is editing, so an open tab no longer keeps a server awake, and reconnects after a pause pick up exactly where they left off.

#### Fixes

- The play display no longer shows a "Still playing?" notice when its live connection pauses after 30 minutes of inactivity. The last scene stays on screen and the display still reconnects on its own when you make an edit or touch the screen.
